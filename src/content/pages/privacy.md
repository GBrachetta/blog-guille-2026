This blog is a personal, independent site. There are no advertising trackers, no surveillance cookies, no third-party behavioral analytics, and no data broker partnerships.

Here is an honest, straightforward explanation of what happens when you visit this site and use its features.

## Comments and Authentication (Remark42)

To allow thoughtful discussions without spam bots or fake accounts, this site uses **Remark42**, an open-source, privacy-focused commenting engine.

Remark42 is **self-hosted on my own private hardware** (a Synology NAS). Your comments and profile details do not pass through commercial tracking platforms or third-party cloud aggregators.

### How Signing In Works

To leave a comment, you can sign in using **Google** or **GitHub**:

- **What data is accessed:** When you authenticate, Remark42 requests only your public profile identity: your public display name, avatar URL, and an internal user ID provided by Google or GitHub.
- **What is NOT accessed:** We never have access to your private emails, password, contacts, Google Drive files, private GitHub repositories, or any other personal account data. Authentication happens entirely on Google’s or GitHub’s secure servers.

### Where Data is Stored

- Your comment text, public username, and avatar URL are stored in a local database on my private server.
- This data is used solely to attribute comments to their authors and display conversations beneath the relevant blog posts.
- It is never sold, shared with third parties, rented, or used for advertising or profiling.

### Essential Cookies & Sessions

Remark42 uses a single essential, secure session token (a JSON Web Token) strictly to keep you logged in while you read, write, or edit your comments. This cookie is purely functional and is never used to track you across other websites.

## Your Rights and Data Control

- **Editing and Deleting:** You can edit or delete your comments directly from the comment interface within the active editing window.
- **Data Erasure:** If you ever wish to have your comments, account record, or profile data permanently removed from my server, send an email to [brachetta@me.com](mailto:brachetta@me.com) and I will wipe them from the database promptly.

## External Links

This site contains links to other websites (musicians, instrument makers, music archives, software documentation, and personal projects). If you click an external link, their respective privacy policies and practices apply.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this site, its infrastructure, or your data, feel free to reach out directly at [brachetta@me.com](mailto:brachetta@me.com).
